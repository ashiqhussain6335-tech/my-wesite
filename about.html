About Us

Engineering the Future of Structures

TopoCore Global llc is a specialist structural and offshore engineering consultancy dedicated to delivering high-quality, code-compliant design solutions across the full project lifecycle. Founded in 2024, we combine deep technical expertise with advanced software tools to serve clients in oil & gas, power, petrochemical, and industrial sectors.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognised centre of excellence for structural and offshore engineering, setting benchmarks in technical rigour, innovation, and project delivery — empowering industries to build safer, smarter, and more efficiently.

Our Mission

We deliver precision-engineered structural solutions through collaborative expertise, cutting-edge analysis, and strict adherence to international standards. Our mission is to provide cost-effective, constructable designs that exceed client expectations while maintaining the highest standards of safety and quality.
